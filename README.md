Your crosshair sways with your steps, kicks when you strike a mob or break a block, and draws back as you charge a bow, crossbow or trident.

- Enable View Bobbing for the walking animation. Toggle individual effects for sneaking, switching items, eating and attacking in the mod settings.
- On Forge 1.20.1 the attack indicator moves with your crosshair while health bars and the hotbar stay in place.

## Installation

Install the JAR on your client and remove the original Crosshair Bobbing JAR. This release targets Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge. Based on Krash220’s Crosshair Bobbing, licensed under GPL-3.0-only.
